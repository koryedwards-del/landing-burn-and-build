Contact & Support

Burn & Build runs in your browser — complete the program questionnaire, pay once ($279), then download your Burn & Build Diet PDF. Here’s how it works and what to do if something goes wrong.

Get started

New program? Start at the program questionnaire, review your answers, pay $279 once through Stripe Checkout (promotion codes accepted at checkout), then download your Burn & Build Diet PDF.

Already purchased? Return to checkout with the email you used at purchase to download your PDF, or check that inbox for your confirmation email.

Email support

Reach us at support@burnandbuilddiet.com

Include the email address you used when creating your program. That helps us find your account quickly.

We typically respond within 24–48 hours.

Checkout & access

Paid but cannot download your diet? Use the same email address in the questionnaire and at Stripe checkout. After payment, return to checkout and enter that email to open your program.

Promotion code? Enter it on the Stripe checkout page. If the total is $0, checkout still completes and unlocks your PDF.

Checkout error? Make sure you’re using a valid card when payment is required. Live promotion codes must be created in live Stripe mode.

Receipts are emailed by Stripe after successful payment.

Using your program

Need your PDF again? Return to checkout after payment to download, or check your email inbox (including spam) for the message with your Burn & Build Diet attached.

Ready to start over? Visit the homepage, complete the questionnaire again, and checkout — each new build creates a new program.

Your Burn & Build Diet PDF is generated after purchase and emailed to you. Save it somewhere you will see it every day.

Daily menu plan worksheet — print a blank copy as often as you like: Menu Plan template (PDF). Page 7 of your diet PDF shows a filled example of how to use it.
