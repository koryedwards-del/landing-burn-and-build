Privacy Policy

Burn & Build is a web-based personalized diet. This policy describes what we collect and how we use it.

What We Collect

When you complete the program questionnaire on our website, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Preferred name
  • Sex
  • Height, age, and current weight
  • Body fat percentage and test method
  • Job activity and life outside work & training
  • Exercise type and hours
  • Waiver signature and date

We use this information to calculate and save your program.

Where Your Data Lives

On our servers. When you complete the questionnaire, we save your email, your answers, your calculated program, and whether your account has paid access so you can download your Burn & Build Diet PDF after purchase.

In your browser. Session and local storage may hold your email and program handoff data so pages load without signing in on every visit. Clearing browser data may require you to enter your email again.

Payment processing. Checkout is handled by Stripe. We do not receive or store your full card number. Stripe provides us with payment confirmation and the email used at checkout.

Service Providers

We use trusted providers to run Burn & Build:

  • Stripe — secure checkout and payment processing
  • Render — hosting for the API that saves your program and delivers your PDF
  • Resend — transactional email delivery after purchase
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for our marketing site and questionnaire

These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service. We do not sell your personal information.

What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not display third-party advertising
  • We do not use third-party analytics or ad trackers on the site
  • We do not store your credit or debit card details

Purchases

Burn & Build is a one-time purchase ($279) through Stripe Checkout on the web. Stripe processes the payment; we receive confirmation so we can unlock your Burn & Build Diet PDF. Promotion codes entered at checkout are applied by Stripe.

Email

Your email is used to save your program, verify purchase, unlock download, and send your Burn & Build Diet PDF after checkout.

Age

Burn & Build is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

Your Choices

You can email us to ask about your data or request deletion of your saved program and contact record. Clearing browser data on your device removes local session data stored there.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated date.
