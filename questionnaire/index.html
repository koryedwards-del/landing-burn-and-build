Step 4

Be realistic about the exercise you will commit to for the next 8 weeks. Conservative is better.

Plan for what you will actually do — not what you wish you would do.
  • All three categories can be 0 if you are not doing that type of activity.

Enter hours per week down to the quarter hour (15 minutes). Overstating exercise lowers your fat servings and makes the plan harder to follow.
