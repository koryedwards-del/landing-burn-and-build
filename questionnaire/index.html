    Your weight and your body composition are used to determine your LBM. LBM, predominantly muscle, is your metabolism. A five pound error in LBM mass will be a one serving difference in daily protein servings. Just a reminder here. You're paying $149 for this program.     Enter body fat as a percentage (not BMI). One decimal is fine — e.g. 24.5. A professional test is worth it if you can get one. Rough reference if you are estimating: many men fall 18–28%; many women 25–35%. When unsure, estimate slightly higher rather than lower.

    % body fat

    Your food plan is built from lean body mass (weight minus fat). Wrong body fat % = wrong servings from day one. Pick the most accurate source you actually have — not the one you wish you had.
