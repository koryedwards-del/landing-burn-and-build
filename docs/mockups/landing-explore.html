How do I lose fat without losing muscle?

Most diets produce weight loss by sacrificing muscle. When muscle goes, metabolism goes down with it. Burn & Build protects lean body mass so your body can keep burning fat.

Can I build muscle while losing fat?

That's body recomposition — losing fat without losing muscle. It depends on eating enough of the right foods for your activity level, not eating less and hoping exercise picks up the slack.

What should I eat to lose fat and build muscle?

Your plan uses servings — not calorie counting — across protein, dairy, grains, starches, vegetables, and fruit. You select foods from the approved list and follow your serving totals for each meal and snack.

How much protein do I need to preserve muscle while losing weight?

Protein servings should be spread fairly even across the day, with a minimum of three protein feedings. At least one-third of daily protein should come from dairy. Your exact totals come from your lean body mass and what you do — not a generic gram target.

How many calories should I eat to lose fat without losing muscle?

You don't count calories on Burn & Build. The Burn Engine calculates your daily servings from your lean body mass, job activity, training, and lifestyle — sized so you eat enough to protect muscle while losing fat.

What is body recomposition?

Body recomposition means losing fat without losing muscle. Burn & Build is a precision body-recomposition program built from your body composition, job activity, workouts, and daily lifestyle — not a one-size-fits-all template.
